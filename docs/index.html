✅ Complete ERP Module Suite (All Live)

PlexusBiz Automate is a full-scale Enterprise Resource Planning system. Every module below is fully implemented with automated tests.

1. Immutable Financial Ledger Live

Double-entry accounting engine with absolute immutability. Cross-domain communication via Transactional Outbox Pattern.

2. NBR VAT & Statutory Tax Live

Bangladesh NBR Mushak forms (6.1, 6.2, 6.3, 9.1). VDS Engine with 15% withholding for statutory services.

3. HCM & Bangladesh Payroll Live

ZKBioTime biometric integration. Progressive NBR tax brackets, Provident Fund, and Gratuity calculations.

4. Procurement & Landed Cost Live

Three-Way Matching (PO vs GRN vs Invoice). Ad Valorem Landed Cost Engine for true COGS.

5. Multi-Warehouse Inventory Live

Spatial mapping (Zone/Aisle/Rack/Bin). FIFO/FEFO batch valuation. TSP routing for warehouse picking.

6. Corporate Governance Live

RJSC compliance (Form XII, Schedule X). HMAC SHA-256 Digital Signatures for tamper-proof document signing.

7. Manufacturing (MRP) Live

Bills of Materials, BOM explosion. Auto-PO generation on shortages. Production Orders with raw material deduction.

8. Asset Management Live

Fixed asset tracking with straight-line depreciation engine. Monthly schedule generation and asset transfers.

9. Quality Control (QC) Live

Inspection criteria evaluation. Auto-generated Non-Conformance Reports with severity scaling.

10. Budget & Forecasting Live

Budget line items with variance analysis. Automatic flagging (warning at 90%, overspent at 100%+).

11. Project Management Live

Projects, tasks, timesheets. Analytics engine with health scoring (on_track / at_risk / critical).

12. Document Management Live

Polymorphic document attachments (attach to PO, Invoice, Project). Versioning and metadata tracking.

13. Audit Trail Live

Universal change tracking for any entity. Logs old/new values, IP, user agent for compliance.

14. Workflow Automation & AI Help Center Live

Automated trigger-based rules (Email, SMS, Notifications). AI Chatbot with fallback to human escalation and CRM tracking.
